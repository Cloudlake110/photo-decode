# Reconstruction Protocol — New Visual, No Filter

## 1. Reconstruct from structure, not pixels

Use the Visual Structure Ledger as the blueprint. The right panel should be drawable even if the original photo were temporarily hidden after analysis.

## 2. Preserve relational truth

Preserve when identity-defining:

- left/right order;
- top/bottom hierarchy;
- relative scale;
- overlap/occlusion;
- direction of movement or gaze;
- support/contact relations;
- repetition density;
- major axes and symmetry/asymmetry.

You may reorganize only to clarify the same structural truth.

## 3. Remove photographic background

For portraits, objects, news, sports, groups, products, architecture/interiors and animals: remove incidental photographic setting.

For landscape/artwork where environment is the subject: convert environment into **flat subject layers**, not a realistic backdrop.

## 4. Source-adaptive rendering

Select rendering behavior from the source itself:

- contour density;
- ornament density;
- amount of internal subdivision;
- hard vs soft shape boundaries;
- geometric vs organic rhythm;
- palette relationships;
- material cues worth retaining.

Do not select a named external style as a shortcut.

## 5. Anti-filter audit

Before finalizing Block 2 ask:

- Did I apply a universal texture to everything?
- Does this look like engraving/woodcut/comic/vector simply because that is an easy transformation?
- Would an unrelated image get nearly the same surface treatment?
- Have I reconstructed relations, or only restyled pixels?

Any “yes” = fail and rebuild.

## 6. Complexity budget

Keep enough structural information to explain the source, but remove enough incidental information to create a strong before/after contrast.

Prefer **3–7 structural groups**, **2–5 important relations**, and **1–3 distinctive rhythms/motifs** rather than hundreds of isolated details.
